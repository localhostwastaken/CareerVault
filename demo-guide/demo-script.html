Q&AQuestions about the demo itself

Is that really live, or a mock-up?
It is our production site, recorded on 16 November. The database, the file storage and the Polygon transactions are all real; you can open the same transactions on PolygonScan today.
Why is it a recording and not live?
So the whole journey fits in ten minutes without waiting on typing or the network. A scripted browser drove the real site in one continuous take, with no edits.
What did Marcus get wrong at the step-up?
One of his three security answers. The letter was not signed until all his details matched.
Why did verification say "timestamp pending" at first?
The letter is valid the moment it is issued. The public timestamp comes with the next batch, which Olivia triggered with "Anchor now". Then hand over to Team Member 4 for the detail.
Why does Bob's letter show 4 of 5 checks?
It is genuine: issued by TechCorp, unchanged, both signatures valid. The fifth check, "not revoked", fails, so the verdict is Revoked.
